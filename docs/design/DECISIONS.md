11. **Bounded own-movement rewind for late commands?** If a late *move* command's lateness window touched no interactions (hits, CC, collisions), the server could apply it on time by re-simulating only that unit, which would remove most remaining corrections. It's a narrow, input-favoring form of lag compensation, so it needs a careful look at abuse (e.g. deliberately delaying inputs).
12. **Champion-vs-champion collision corrections:** all champions block each other (D23, after R03 reversed D20), so crowd bumps cause ~4 corrections > 15 u per player-minute among 10 click-spamming bots at 80 ms, because another player's next click is unpredictable. Remaining options: accept (crowd bumping is rarer in real play), or shrink enemy-champion proxies slightly to bias toward late blocks. The enemy case is now assumed to match the allied one measured in R03. Slice 4: the same unpredictability shows when chasing a champion with an attack order (~5–8 corrections > 15 u per player-minute in the lab duel; mean visible correction stays ≤ 0.5 u).
13. ~~**Snapshot bandwidth**~~: resolved by D33 (deltas and path coasting).
14. **Champion collision radius:** R03 measured contact at ~75–85 u center to center, which suggests ~40 u per champion rather than 35 u *(start)*. Within measurement error; revisit with a capture of two champions body-blocking at known positions.
15. **Art sources in git:** commit `.blend` files directly (simple for contributors; ~1–5 MB each for low-poly) or use Git LFS (keeps clone size down as the roster grows, but costs bandwidth quota and adds a setup step)? The plan is direct commits until the repo passes ~1 GB ([10 §8.1](10-characters-and-animation.md#81-layout)).
16. **Predictable crits:** rolling crits at windup start from a per-champion seeded stream would let our own predicted attack play `attack_crit` from frame one, but it also lets a modified client know the next crit in advance. The alternative is a server-rolled crit with a crossfade into the crit clip ~100 ms in. Decide when crit items land (M4).
