| D27 | 2026-10-06 | **Attack rules:** in range when `distance ≤ attack range + target gameplay radius` (02 §1, center to edge), judged against start-of-tick positions like collision; attack orders and attack-move never target a unit the attacker's team can't see (the server hands the sim a per-team hidden list each tick); turrets are untargetable for now | Order-independent and reproducible by prediction from proxies, which only exist for visible units, so client and server agree on targets | Accepted |
| D28 | 2026-10-06 | **Combat prediction:** own casts, attack windups and timers, dashes, blinks and shields are predicted; damage, CC, deaths and respawn positions come from the server and reconcile like any state change. | D29 | 2026-10-06 | **Fog for combat events:** casts and bolt launches only if the caster is visible; damage, deaths, respawns, dashes, blinks and shields only if the unit's position is visible (a blink seen only at its end doesn't reveal where it started); enemy areas once their center is visible. | D30 | 2026-10-06 | **Blind playtest protocol (M1):** testers play short rounds (default 10 × 60 s) under hidden conditions: added latency (0 / 30 / 60 / 80 / 120 ms RTT profiles), crossed with own-missile Option A/B and minion bubble on/off. ## Open questions

1. **Project name:** keep "MFTR" as the public name or only as a codename? And our own term for "champions"?
2. ~~Numeric determinism~~: resolved by D19.
3. **Transport:** `renet` + netcode protocol vs. `quinn` (QUIC) vs. our own AEAD layer on the existing UDP code. Decide with the lobby/token work, before M2 (see D15).
4. **ECS vs. arenas** for `mftr-sim` (`bevy_ecs` standalone / `hecs` / hand-rolled). `Vec<Unit>` plus an O(n²) broadphase is fine for the sandbox's ~70 units; add a spatial grid before full waves (M2).
5. **Mod scripting language** (post-M3): Rhai, Lua, or WASM?
6. **Cosmetics policy:** community skins allowed? Under what readability review? Client-side "show default models" toggle?
7. **Funding:** donations (Open Collective / Liberapay), grants, or optional paid convenience (e.g. hosted servers), with nothing that affects gameplay.
8. **Tick rate:** stay at 30 Hz or test 60 Hz in M1? (The plan is to measure, not guess.)
9. **Godot renderer default:** Mobile vs. Forward+ on desktop. Measure on the minimum-spec iGPU in M1.
10. **Tuning baseline:** how closely to match reference-game numbers (stats, gold values, timers) for the first playable, before diverging.
11. **Bounded own-movement rewind for late commands?** If a late *move* command's lateness window touched no interactions (hits, CC, collisions), the server could apply it on time by re-simulating only that unit, which would remove most remaining corrections. It's a narrow, input-favoring form of lag compensation, so it needs a careful look at abuse (e.g. deliberately delaying inputs).
12. **Champion-vs-champion collision corrections:** all champions block each other (D23, after R03 reversed D20), so crowd bumps cause ~4 corrections > 15 u per player-minute among 10 click-spamming bots at 80 ms, because another player's next click is unpredictable. Remaining options: accept (crowd bumping is rarer in real play), or shrink enemy-champion proxies slightly to bias toward late blocks. The enemy case is now assumed to match the allied one measured in R03. Slice 4: the same unpredictability shows when chasing a champion with an attack order (~5–8 corrections > 15 u per player-minute in the lab duel; mean visible correction stays ≤ 0.5 u).
13. **Snapshot bandwidth:** implement path-coasting + baseline deltas (03b §6) now that ~70 units cost ~25 KB/s per player.
14. **Champion collision radius:** R03 measured contact at ~75–85 u center to center, which suggests ~40 u per champion rather than 35 u *(start)*. Within measurement error; revisit with a capture of two champions body-blocking at known positions.
