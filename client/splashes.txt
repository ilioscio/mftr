# What MFTR stands for today: one is rolled each time the client starts and shown under the logo.
#
#   - One per line. Blank lines and lines starting with # are skipped.
#   - The capitalized words spell M F T R; lowercase filler is free ("Moba For The Rest of us").
#     tests/splashes_test.gd checks this, along with duplicates and length (60 characters at most).
#   - A line starting with a date is a holiday line: on those days it's rolled instead of the
#     everyday ones. [12-25] is a day every year, [12-20..12-26] a range (it may wrap the new year),
#     and [2027-02-06..2027-02-08] pins the year, for holidays that move.
#   - The client won't show the same line again until it has shown about half of the others.
