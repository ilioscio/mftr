# Brow styles (22 §3.1, BW2): what `looks.brows.style` names. The forge shapes each brow strip
# of a humanoid base (mftr_forge::brows) by its style, then by the recipe's sizes.
#
# Format: `[id] Name: a line on it` starts a style; `joined` on a line of its own lets the brow
# run in to meet its twin over the nose. Then two lines of seven values each, at even steps from
# the brow's inner end (first) to its tip (last):
#   thick  how heavy the brow is there, 1 being the default brow's (6.5 mm, top to bottom);
#   arch   how far its middle line rises there, in mm (below 0 it falls).
# The first style is the default. The creator lists them in this order.
