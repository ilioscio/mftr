# The face sliders as presets on the face bones (21 §2.7, FR3, D76).
#
# A recipe's `shape: (nose_reach: 0.6, …)` moves the face bones by 0.6 × the preset below
# (negative values the opposite way), summed over every slider and clamped to each bone's limits
# (mftr_forge::face::LIMITS). The forge and the Blender preview both read this file.
#
# Format: `[id] description` starts a preset; `[id+] …` is one-sided (values below 0 do
# nothing). Each line under it: `bone kind x y z` with kind t (translation, 0.1 mm), r (rotation
# vector, 0.01°) or s (scale, log2 in 0.001 steps), in the bone's own frame: +x toward the
# character's right, +y out of the face, +z up; except the jaw's, which leans along the jaw toward
# the chin (+y forward and down, +z back and down: straight down is y 0.68, z 0.74). A trailing `mirror` on a `_l` bone adds the
# `_r` bone mirrored (x and the rotation's y and z flip).
